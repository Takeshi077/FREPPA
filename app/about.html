Brief History of FREPPA Group of Schools

The vision to commence FREPPA GROUP OF SCHOOLS started with the acquisition of land and the necessary documentation in 2011. This was completed in 2011 and the survey plan, Architectural designs and C of O were obtained.

The construction of the first 6 classrooms started in 2018 and was completed in 2019 and the advertisement began for the 2019/2020 academic session. Upon resumption, the School commenced operations with Seven (7) pupils and five (5) Teachers.

Other materials and documentation such as the School Uniform, Text-boxes, the School's logo, Anthem, Vision, Mission and Core-Values were achieved through consultations with Stakeholders in Education and the Ondo State Ministry of Education.

The School Proprietress, who is also educationally qualified took time to engage Teachers who have backgrounds in Education and with vast experiences to teach in the School.

Today, the School has consistently grown in infrastructure and in population through effective Management and Supervision.
